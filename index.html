Why Do We Pay for Internet?

The internet is powered by enormous physical infrastructure, electricity, repairs and people working around the clock.

🚢

Ocean Repair Ships

Specialized ships sail the ocean floor to locate and repair damaged undersea fiber-optic cables, often taking weeks and costing millions per mission.

📡

Towers & Data Centers

Mobile towers require constant power and battery backup. Massive data centers consume enormous electricity and advanced cooling systems to keep servers running non-stop.

🛡️

Cybersecurity 24/7

Teams of security engineers monitor and defend the global network from hackers and cyber threats around the clock, every single day of the year.
